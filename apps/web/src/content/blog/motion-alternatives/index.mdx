The best Motion alternative for most people is **Open Sunsama**. It is an open-source daily planner with time blocking that works with any AI agent. You get AI planning you can see, and your data stays yours: self-host it or use its public API.

We build Open Sunsama, so we put it first — here is why, and where others fit better. If you want software to schedule your day, **FlowSavvy** is the closest match for one person. **Reclaim** is the best auto-scheduler for teams.

**Morgen** suggests a plan you approve, and **Sunsama** guides a calm, manual plan. This Motion alternative guide covers eight apps, with prices checked in September 2026.

- **Best overall:** Open Sunsama. Open source, self-hostable, and one MCP link for Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor. You or your agent plan, and nothing moves on its own.
- **Closest to Motion for one person:** FlowSavvy. Real auto-scheduling, full phone apps, a free plan.
- **Best auto-scheduler for teams:** Reclaim. It schedules tasks, habits and meetings, and lives in Google Calendar or Outlook.

## The real choice: autopilot or control

Motion now calls itself an "AI SuperApp for work", with projects, docs and notes. Its core is still simple. You list tasks with deadlines and lengths, and it builds your calendar.

When a meeting runs over, it rebuilds the rest of the day.

That works well for some people. Others leave for the same reasons again and again:

- **It feels like a black box.** One user said you "have to reverse engineer how your own week got created".
- **It takes the day out of your hands.** Tasks jump around, and you stop trusting the plan.
- **Setup is heavy.** It takes real work before the plans feel right.
- **The price is high for one person.** There is no free plan, and you pay per seat.
- **Your AI agent can't easily reach it.** Motion has a REST API, but we found no official MCP server for Claude or ChatGPT.

Our top pick, Open Sunsama, sits in the middle. You plan, or your own AI agent plans when you ask. The other Motion alternatives fall into three groups:

1. **Auto-schedulers** do what Motion does, often for less money: Reclaim, FlowSavvy, SkedPal and ClickUp.
2. **AI assistants that ask first** suggest a plan and wait for your OK: Morgen and Akiflow's chat assistant.
3. **Manual planners** keep you in charge: Sunsama guides your plan each morning.

## Auto-schedulers: the closest Motion alternatives

Pick from this group if you liked Motion's autopilot and want it cheaper, simpler or better for teams.

## 2. FlowSavvy is the closest Motion alternative for one person

**Verdict:** the best drop-in replacement if auto-scheduling is the part of Motion you want to keep.

**Best for:** solo users who want the calendar built for them.

FlowSavvy does Motion's core job. You add tasks with a length, a deadline and a priority.

It places them in your calendar and moves them when plans change. It skips Motion's team and project extras.

**What works**

- Auto-scheduling with priorities, deadlines, work hours and repeating tasks.
- Full iOS and Android apps, plus two-way sync with Google, Outlook and iCloud.
- A free plan, and an API and MCP server on the Pro plan.

**Where it falls short**

- No team features.
- No desktop apps. On a computer you use the web app.
- The same trust problem as Motion: the app, not you, decides the order.

**Pick it if** you want Motion's autopilot for yourself, without the team price.

## 3. Reclaim is the best auto-scheduler for teams

**Verdict:** the best Motion alternative if your whole team lives in Google Calendar or Outlook.

**Best for:** teams that want focus time, habits and meetings scheduled for them.

Reclaim adds a smart layer on top of your calendar. It books time for tasks, habits and focus, and moves them by priority when meetings land. It also finds meeting times across a team.

Dropbox bought Reclaim in August 2024. Reclaim 2.0, now in private beta, suggests tasks instead of placing them.

**What works**

- Auto-scheduling for tasks, habits, focus time and team meetings.
- Task sync with Asana, ClickUp, Jira, Linear, Todoist and Google Tasks (most need a paid plan).
- An official MCP server, and an AI chat that shows changes before they happen.

**Where it falls short**

- No native phone apps. You use the web app on your phone.
- No project views like Motion's. Tasks live in a flat list or in your task app.
- It only works with Google Calendar and Outlook.

**Pick it if** you want Motion-style scheduling across a team. We compare them in [Reclaim AI vs Motion vs Sunsama](/blog/reclaim-ai-vs-motion-vs-sunsama), and list more options in our [Reclaim alternatives](/blog/reclaim-ai-alternatives) guide.

## 4. SkedPal gives you the most control over the algorithm

**Verdict:** the best Motion alternative if Motion felt too simple, not too complex.

**Best for:** planners who want to set exact rules for when each task can run.

SkedPal is an auto-scheduler with deep settings. You can say "one hour this week, mornings only, no more than 30 minutes a day". It builds the plan when you click "Update schedule", not on its own.

**What works**

- Very fine rules for time windows, splits and limits.
- Nested subtasks and outlines for big projects.
- You decide when the schedule updates, which cuts surprise moves.

**Where it falls short**

- A dense screen and a steep learning curve.
- The phone apps are companions, not full planners.
- No free plan.

**Pick it if** you want an auto-scheduler you can tune until it thinks like you.

## 5. ClickUp is the pick if you used Motion for projects

**Verdict:** the best Motion alternative if the project side of Motion mattered more than the calendar.

**Best for:** teams that need boards, docs, comments and assignees.

ClickUp is a full project tool that has added basic auto-scheduling. In its planner, you click a task and it drops into your next free slot. It won't rebuild your whole week like Motion does.

**What works**

- Almost every project view you could want, plus docs and chat.
- Strong team features and many integrations.
- A free plan, plus apps for web, desktop and phone.

**Where it falls short**

- Auto-scheduling is basic next to Motion's.
- So many features that it takes weeks to learn.

**Pick it if** you need a team project tool first and a planner second.

## Planners that keep you in control

Pick from this group if Motion's autopilot was the problem. These apps suggest, guide or wait for you.

## 6. Morgen suggests a plan and waits for your OK

**Verdict:** the best Motion alternative if you want AI help without surprises.

**Best for:** people with many calendars and task apps.

Morgen puts all your calendars and tasks in one view. Its AI Planner suggests where tasks should go.

You accept, change or reject the plan. When a meeting lands on a task, Morgen asks before it moves anything.

**What works**

- Calendar sync for Google, Outlook, iCloud and Fastmail.
- Two-way sync with Todoist, Notion, Linear, ClickUp and more.
- An AI Planner that never moves a task without your OK.
- Apps for Mac, Windows, Linux, iOS and Android.

**Where it falls short**

- Not fully automatic, by design.
- No project views like Motion's.

**Pick it if** you want AI to do the math and you to make the call.

## 7. Sunsama guides a calm, manual plan

**Verdict:** the best Motion alternative if you want to plan with intent, not hand your day to software.

**Best for:** people who felt stressed by a calendar that kept moving.

Sunsama is Motion's opposite. Each morning it walks you through what fits today.

You drag tasks onto your calendar yourself. It warns you when you plan more hours than you have.

**What works**

- A daily planning ritual and a shutdown ritual.
- Two-way sync with about 20 task tools, plus Google, Outlook and iCloud calendars.
- An AI assistant called Sunny and an [official MCP server](https://help.sunsama.com/docs/mcp-model-context-protocol) for Claude and ChatGPT.

**Where it falls short**

- No full auto-scheduling. Its Autoplan rules are still in beta.
- No public REST API.
- Some users find the ritual turns into a daily chore.

**Pick it if** you want a calm, realistic day that you choose. See our full [Motion vs Sunsama](/blog/motion-vs-sunsama) comparison.

## 8. Akiflow pulls every task into one inbox

**Verdict:** the best Motion alternative if your tasks start in Slack, email and five other tools.

**Best for:** people who want fast capture and manual time blocking.

Akiflow collects tasks from your other apps into one inbox. You drag them onto your calendar, mostly with the keyboard.

Its chat assistant, Aki, moves tasks when you ask. A Schedule Optimizer, added in June 2026, re-plans the rest of your day when a task runs long.

**What works**

- A universal inbox for tasks from email, Slack and many work tools.
- Keyboard shortcuts for almost every action.
- A chat assistant for quick changes like "move today's tasks to Monday", plus an official MCP server.

**Where it falls short**

- No free plan.
- The phone apps get mixed reviews.
- It is not a full auto-scheduler like Motion.

**Pick it if** capture is your problem, not scheduling. Our [Akiflow vs Sunsama vs Motion](/blog/akiflow-vs-sunsama-vs-motion) post goes deeper.
