## The real difference: who decides your day

Motion and Sunsama solve the same problem from opposite ends. Both put tasks on your calendar. The question is who picks the time slot.

**Motion decides for you.** You add a task with a deadline, a priority and a length.

Motion's AI finds a slot for it. When a meeting lands on top of it, Motion moves the task and everything after it.

**Sunsama asks you to decide.** Each morning you pick today's tasks, estimate each one and drag it onto the calendar. Sunsama adds up your estimates and warns you when the day won't fit.

Neither way is right for everyone. Some people feel freed when an app makes the calls. Others feel lost when they can't see why their week looks the way it does.

## A typical Tuesday in Motion and in Sunsama

Here is how a typical busy Tuesday looks in each app, based on how each one describes its planning flow. It is an example, not a measurement: six tasks, three meetings, and one meeting that runs 45 minutes late.

**In Sunsama,** you start with a morning plan of about ten minutes. You pull tasks from Todoist and Gmail and set estimates. The total says 9 hours, so you push two tasks to Wednesday before the day begins.

When the meeting runs late, you drag the next two blocks down by hand. It takes a minute, and you know exactly where everything is.

**In Motion,** there is no morning plan. You added the tasks with deadlines the night before, so the calendar is already full when you wake up.

When the meeting runs late, Motion moves the rest of the day on its own. A low-priority task may slide to Thursday without you noticing.

Both days can end with the same work done. Users tend to call the Sunsama day more deliberate. They call the Motion day lighter, but say you have to check where things went.

## Where Motion wins

### It handles chaos for you

If your calendar changes ten times a day, Motion saves real effort. You never have to rebuild the afternoon by hand. One Hacker News user said Motion felt like putting on mental [glasses](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44391813).

### It runs projects, not just days

Motion has grown into a work app with projects, tasks, docs and dashboards. Business AI adds Gantt charts, capacity planning and time tracking. Sunsama doesn't try to do this.

### It has a public API

Motion offers a [REST API](https://docs.usemotion.com/) with API keys. You can script it or connect it to other tools. Sunsama has no public API, though it does have an official MCP server.

## Where Sunsama wins

### It keeps you in control

Nothing moves unless you move it. That matters to people who distrust auto-schedulers. Critics of Motion-style tools say you can end up having to [reverse engineer](https://iphonecleaner.net/t/can-anyone-share-an-honest-motion-app-review-or-experience/2002) how your own week was built.

### It stops overplanning

Sunsama's workload total is its quiet superpower. You see the hours add up before you commit. Paired with planned vs actual time, it teaches you what a realistic day looks like.

### It pulls tasks from your other tools

Sunsama imports tasks from Asana, ClickUp, Jira, Linear, Notion, Todoist, Trello, Monday.com, GitHub, Gmail, Slack and more. You keep your tools and add a daily plan on top. Motion works best when your tasks live inside Motion.

### It has official AI agent access

In August 2026, Sunsama's AI assistant, Sunny, and its [MCP server](https://help.sunsama.com/docs/mcp-model-context-protocol) left beta. You can plan your day in chat or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor. Motion's MCP options for its calendar app are community-built.

### It is easier to try

Sunsama gives you 14 days with no card. Motion gives you 7 days and needs a card up front.

## AI in 2026: both have it, in different ways

Older comparisons say "Motion has AI and Sunsama doesn't". That is out of date.

- **Motion's AI does the scheduling.** It also offers AI chat, an AI writer and meeting notes. These use a monthly credit allowance, and heavy use can cost extra.
- **Sunsama's AI helps you plan.** Sunny can create and move tasks, run your daily plan, sort a brain dump into your backlog and sum up last week. You still make the calls.

If you want an agent like Claude to manage your planner, Sunsama is the easier path today. It has an official, hosted MCP server with OAuth. With Motion, you use its API key or a community MCP server.
